ANALYSIS GUIDELINES - CONTEXT-SPECIFIC RULES

1. EXECUTION ENVIRONMENT ASSESSMENT:

   - Short duration: May indicate quick execution or failure
   - Long duration: May indicate persistence or sleeping

2. PAYLOAD EXTRACTION ASSESSMENT:
   - No payloads: Could indicate fileless malware or extraction failure
   - Multiple payloads: Suggests staged delivery or dropper behavior
   - Packed payloads: Indicates obfuscation attempts
   - Specific process sources: Contextualize based on process legitimacy

3. PROCESSING METRICS INTERPRETATION:
   - High processing ratio: May indicate complex analysis requirements
   - Memory-heavy processing: Suggests memory analysis was significant
   - High zero-time detections: Indicates known malicious patterns
   - Phase distribution: Reveals where analysis effort was focused

4. ANOMALY DETECTION LOGIC:

   - Long duration + low processing: May indicate sleeping/waiting
   - Zero payloads + high detections: Static detection without dynamic behavior
